# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Last updated / 最后更新：2026-10-15**

## English

Reclink ("the Extension") records your screen, tab, or camera and saves recordings locally in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server.

- **No data collection.** All recordings, screenshots, and OCR results are processed and stored entirely on your device (browser local storage / OPFS). Nothing is uploaded.
- **No analytics, no tracking.** The Extension contains no analytics SDKs, no trackers, and makes no requests to third-party servers.
- **Permissions.** Permissions requested (tab capture, screen capture, camera, microphone, clipboard) are used solely to provide the recording features you explicitly invoke.
- **Deletion.** You can delete any recording from the Extension's library at any time; uninstalling the Extension removes all locally stored data.

Contact: replace-with-your-contact@example.com

## 中文

Reclink（"本扩展"）用于录制屏幕、标签页或摄像头画面，并将录制内容保存在您的浏览器本地。我们不收集、传输或在任何服务器上存储您的个人数据。

- **不收集数据。** 所有录制、截图及 OCR 结果均仅在您的设备上（浏览器本地存储 / OPFS）处理和保存，不上传任何内容。
- **无统计、无追踪。** 本扩展不包含任何统计 SDK、追踪器，也不会向第三方服务器发起请求。
- **权限说明。** 所申请的权限（标签页捕获、屏幕捕获、摄像头、麦克风、剪贴板）仅用于实现您主动触发的录制功能。
- **数据删除。** 您可随时在扩展媒体库中删除任意录制内容；卸载扩展将删除所有本地数据。

联系方式：replace-with-your-contact@example.com
