Privacy Policy

Short version: Reclink has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything you record or capture is processed on your device and stays on your device unless you choose to download or copy it.

1. Data stored locally on your device

All of the following live in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB / chrome.storage) and never leave your computer:

  • Recorded videos and screenshots
  • OCR-recognized text
  • Your settings (resolution, export format, mic/camera defaults)
  • Library entries (metadata about your recordings)

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

2. Data the extension reads

While recording or capturing, Reclink reads the pixels and audio of exactly what you chose to record — the current tab, a screen/window picked in the native picker, a region you select, or your camera. This content is written directly into the recording file on your device. It is not transmitted, logged, or analyzed anywhere.

3. Permissions and why each is needed

PermissionPurpose
offscreenRuns the recording engine (MediaRecorder) in a background document
tabCaptureCaptures the current tab's video/audio when you choose "record tab"
downloadsSaves recordings/screenshots when you click Download
storageStores your library and settings locally
scripting / tabs / activeTabInject the control bar, region picker and annotation tools into the page you are capturing — only on your explicit action
clipboardWriteCopy screenshots or OCR text when you click Copy
notificationsNotify you if a capture fails, or show the recording countdown when a page cannot be scripted
optional: clipboardReadRead a clipboard image only when you use "annotate clipboard image"
optional host (<all_urls>)Full-page / region screenshots need scripting access to the site you are capturing; requested on demand, not granted at install

4. Third parties

None. Reclink contains no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no remote code. The OCR engine (tesseract.js WebAssembly) and its language packs are bundled inside the extension and run entirely offline.

5. Children's privacy

Reclink does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in the "Effective" date above and in the extension's release notes.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at the extension's repository, or reply through the Chrome Web Store listing.
